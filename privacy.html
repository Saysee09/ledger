Privacy Policy · Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version

  • Your money data stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive. Nobody else can see it, including the people who make Brokeless.
  • There is no Brokeless server. The app talks directly to Google on your behalf.
  • Brokeless can only open the files it created in your Drive. It cannot see your other files, photos or emails.
  • We don't sell, share or show ads with your data. Ever.
  • You can delete everything at any time.

1. What Brokeless is

Brokeless is a personal money tracker for expenses, income, accounts, cards, loans, goals and investments. It is free and made by an independent developer in India.

2. What we store, and where

  • On your phone: the entries you add (amounts, categories, notes, accounts, dates), your settings and your app lock. This copy lets Brokeless work offline.
  • In your Google Drive: when you sign in with Google, Brokeless creates a spreadsheet called "Brokeless" in your Drive and keeps it in sync. It belongs to you, like any other file in your Drive.
  • Bill photos you attach are saved on your phone (Pictures/Brokeless) and, if you choose, in your own Drive.

We, the makers of Brokeless, do not run any server that receives or stores your financial data.

3. Google sign-in and Google data

  • When you sign in, Brokeless asks Google for your email address and name (to show which account is signed in) and for permission to create and manage only the files Brokeless makes in your Drive (the drive.file permission).
  • Brokeless cannot read, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • Brokeless's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can remove Brokeless's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Bank SMS (Android app)

  • If you allow it, the Android app reads bank and card transaction messages on your phone to suggest entries for you. It ignores OTPs and personal messages.
  • Messages are read on your phone only. The amount, payee and date of a transaction (and the bank message itself, so you can check it) are kept with your entries, on your phone and in your own Drive.
  • You can turn this off anytime in Android Settings → Apps → Brokeless → Permissions.

5. Feedback you send us

If you use Send feedback, we receive your message, the app version, your phone model and an anonymous install code, plus your email only if you type it in for a reply. We use this only to fix problems and improve Brokeless, and never share it. Feedback never includes your money data. Ask us to delete your feedback anytime through Send feedback.

6. App updates

The Android app checks GitHub (where Brokeless is published) for new versions. This request contains no personal information.

7. Security

  • You can lock Brokeless with your fingerprint, face, PIN, password or pattern.
  • Data travels between your phone and Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Keep your phone and Google account secure: anyone with access to them could see your Brokeless data.

8. Deleting your data

  • From your phone: Settings → Sign out, or uninstall the app.
  • From your Drive: delete the "Brokeless" spreadsheet in Google Drive (and empty the Drive bin).
  • Access: remove Brokeless at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because we never hold your money data, there is nothing left with us after you do this.

9. Children

Brokeless is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from children.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top, and mention it in the app's "What's new". Questions or requests: use Settings → Send feedback in the app.

Brokeless is an independent app and is not affiliated with Google or any bank.
