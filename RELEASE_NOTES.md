## Ledger v8 · What's new

## New
- **In-app updates**: get a notification when a new version is ready, see what changed, and tap Install now
- **Ask Ledger** ✨: ask "Food last month?", "Can I afford ₹50,000?", "When did I last pay rent?", answered privately on your phone
- **Loan & EMI calculator** in Plan: EMI, total interest, prepay savings, and whether it fits your budget
- **Home-screen widgets**: Free to spend + quick add, This month, Needs you, Money Pulse
- **🔔 Notification centre**: bank SMS to sort, card dues, bills, EMIs and tips in one place
- **Money Pulse v2**: career profile, deep dive into your habits, badges and a profile % meter
- **Sell / withdraw investments** with profit or loss worked out (IPO refunds too)
- **Pay back Mom's money**: spends "for me" from held money are tracked as owed
- **Credit card bills**: dues stay out of your balance, Pay button, reminder from the 28th
- **SMS payee memory**: your own names (Breakfast, Ticket) are remembered for each payee

## Better
- One opening screen: the lock screen shows your PIN with a "Use fingerprint" button
- Lock with phone lock, PIN, password or pattern, and choose when it locks again
- Settings split into short pages
- Amounts in short form: 40K, 1.25L, 2.5Cr
- Free to spend now leaves out money kept in goals, with a Withdraw button on each goal
- Drive: everything in one "Ledger App" folder, backups named by date, delete old backups from the app

## Fixed
- Salary box in Budget plan no longer shows NaN
- Screens no longer spill off the side with large phone text
- Editing an account no longer clears its WhatsApp number and SMS digits
