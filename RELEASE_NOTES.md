## Ledger v8.5 · What's new

## New
- **🎁 Monthly wrap**: your month as swipeable story cards. See how spending compares with last month, where it went, your habits, your biggest moment, how much you kept and your Money Pulse. **Share** or **save ⬇** any card as a picture. Pictures never show amounts, and "₹ Amounts" shows them only on your screen. Open it from Insights, or from the card on the home screen in the first week of each month
- **Bills whose price changes later** (Plan → Bills → a bill → "Price changes later"): e.g. Google ₹151 for 3 payments, then ₹650. Auto-log, Calendar, the bell and your budget use the right price for each date, and the bill switches to the new price by itself
- **Repeating costs in your budget**: every bill, SIP and EMI is now counted as money that will be spent. Bills every 3 months or yearly are set aside monthly (₹899 every 3 months = ₹300 a month)
- **My plans checks your plan**: a new "Repeating costs" card shows which bills, SIPs and EMIs your plan covers (✓), which are missing (⚠️) and which are too small, with one tap to add the missing ones. A catch-all item like "🧾 Bills" counts if it is big enough. Saved plans warn you too, and each plan now has its own month
- **Categories & budgets** show how much of each budget your repeating bills already use
